Delete your PakFasal account

App: PakFasal · Package: pk.pakfasal.app

If you created an account in PakFasal, you can request permanent deletion of your account and associated app data. Account freezing is not used as a substitute.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PakFasal and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your Firebase Authentication user and cloud data we store for the app (profile document, sensor readings, crop plantings) plus local auth preferences on the device.

Option 2 — Request by email (outside the app)

Email us from the address linked to your PakFasal account. We will verify ownership and delete the account and associated data.

To: support@pakfasal.app
Subject: PakFasal Account Deletion
Include: the email used to sign up, and (if known) your display name.

Send deletion request

Typical processing time: within 7 days after we confirm the request. Some backups or third-party logs (e.g. ads/crash providers) may take longer to expire under their policies.
