PakFasal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026 · App: PakFasal (pk.pakfasal.app)

PakFasal (“we”, “our”, “the app”) helps Pakistani farmers with weather, soil advice, learning, marketplace discovery, crop calendars, and related tools. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

1

Data we collect

  • Account information: name/display name, email address, and user ID when you register with email/password or Google Sign-In.
  • Profile photo (optional): if you upload a photo, it is stored with our image host (Cloudinary) and linked in your profile.
  • Location: approximate and/or precise location when you allow it, to show local weather and advisories. You may also search by city instead of using GPS.
  • Farming data you enter: sensor/soil readings, crop plantings/calendar entries, and similar inputs saved to your account.
  • Microphone (optional): used only when you enable voice input (e.g. Ask AI). Audio is processed for speech recognition and is not sold.
  • Camera / photos (optional): used only to pick or capture a profile photo.
  • Diagnostics: crash and error reports via Firebase Crashlytics (device/app info, and a user ID when signed in) to improve stability.
  • Advertising identifiers: when ads are shown (Google AdMob), Google may use advertising/device identifiers according to Google’s policies. During development we may use Google’s test ad units.
  • Local preferences: language, saved weather cities, biometric lock preference, and similar settings stored on your device.
2

How we use data

  • Provide and personalize farming features (weather, advice, calendar, profile).
  • Authenticate you and secure your account.
  • Improve reliability (crash diagnostics).
  • Show advertising (AdMob) to support the free app.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell your personal information.

3

Sharing with service providers

We share data only as needed to run the app:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, Firestore database, Crashlytics.
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose Google login.
  • Google AdMob — ads and related advertising identifiers.
  • Cloudinary — optional profile image hosting.
  • OpenWeather / weather APIs — location coordinates or city queries to fetch forecasts.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and our configuration.

4

Permissions

  • Location — weather for your area.
  • Camera / photos — profile photo only.
  • Microphone — voice input only when you use it.
  • Notifications — crop calendar reminders (if enabled).
  • Biometric — optional app lock on your device.

You can deny or revoke permissions in system settings; some features may then be limited.

5

Data retention & security

Account and farming data are kept while your account is active. We use industry-standard transport security (HTTPS/TLS) for network traffic. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

6

Your choices — access & deletion

  • In-app: Profile → Delete account removes your Firebase Auth account and associated cloud data we store for the app (profile, sensor readings, crop plantings, and local auth artifacts).
  • Outside the app: request deletion at Account deletion or email support@pakfasal.app.

After deletion, residual backup copies may take a short time to clear from provider systems. Ads/analytics partners may retain limited identifiers under their own policies.

7

Children

PakFasal is intended for farmers and general adult users. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8

International processing

Our service providers may process data in data centers outside Pakistan. By using the app you understand that your information may be transferred for the purposes described above.

9

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
